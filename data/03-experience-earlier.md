# Experience before Hypersonic, and education

## Backend and infrastructure engineer, independent network infrastructure (Dec 2023 – Mar 2025)

From December 2023 to March 2025, Arash worked independently and remotely as a backend and infrastructure engineer, designing and operating distributed network infrastructure on a self-managed fleet of more than 20 servers spread across several providers: AWS (EC2 and Lightsail), OVH, Hetzner and DigitalOcean. He routed traffic across distributed nodes, built failover for when network paths degraded, and set up monitoring to catch drops before users noticed. He wrote the tooling mainly in Go: high-concurrency TCP forwarding and encrypted tunnelling services that handled thousands of simultaneous connections on commodity VPS hardware.

## Network infrastructure: security, monitoring and automation (Dec 2023 – Mar 2025)

In his independent network infrastructure work, Arash designed encrypted tunnel and secure gateway architectures, including DDoS mitigation. He built management dashboards for provisioning and fleet administration, and real-time traffic monitoring dashboards with anomaly detection across the fleet. He automated account provisioning, renewals and billing through Telegram bot interfaces, including cryptocurrency payment handling.

## Network infrastructure: open source and what Arash learned (Dec 2023 – Mar 2025)

Arash open-sourced the core tunnelling tools from his independent network infrastructure work, with cross-platform builds from CI. This work led to sluice, his Go tool for multiplexed, TLS-pinned TCP tunnels with Prometheus metrics, and DirectPortForward-Go, a high-performance TCP port forwarder.

What Arash learned from this work: how high-volume traffic behaves in practice, connection management, failover design, and running systems without a team behind him.

## Freelance full-stack developer (2020 – Jul 2023)

From 2020 to July 2023, Arash worked remotely as a self-employed freelance full-stack developer. He delivered more than 10 end-to-end production projects for clients, including e-commerce platforms, dashboards, automation tools and landing pages, plus web and mobile applications. He worked mainly in the Node.js and React ecosystem (Node.js, React, Next.js, React Native), with PostgreSQL, MySQL and MongoDB behind most projects. As a freelancer, Arash managed the full project lifecycle, from architecture and scoping through production deployment.

## Freelance work with Idehweb on Nodeeweb (2020 – Jul 2023)

As part of his freelance work, Arash worked with Idehweb, a website and application development company, on its product Nodeeweb, a no-code/low-code MERN framework (Node.js, React, MongoDB). Arash was a member of the Idehweb team that built Nodeeweb, and worked on its visual drag-and-drop page builder and its CMS and CRM modules. Nodeeweb powers production websites including Gameboss (https://gameboss.shop), an online store for game consoles and game discs; Titipluse (https://titipluse.com); and Gomrok24 (https://gomrok24.com), a customs-services platform for tariff lookup, export valuation and shipping.

## Freelance work through Bardsoftware (2020 – Jul 2023)

As part of his freelance work, Arash worked with Bardsoftware, an agency that took on multiple clients and distributed the work to developers like him. Through Bardsoftware, Arash built the front end for Bridgit's Books (https://bridgitaudio.com), a small business's online service for streaming and buying audiobooks, and for My ESA Doctor (https://myesadoctor.com), an online service for obtaining emotional support animal (ESA) letters from licensed therapists. He also built the React Native mobile app for Melk Pro (https://melk.pro), a real-estate listings and consultancy business, and published it on regional Android app stores.
